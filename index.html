Before You Go

  • Book Vintgar Gorge online for Mon Jun 8, ~9:00 slot — vintgar.si. Morning slots in June sell out.
  • Reserve dinner at Gostilna pod Lipco (Bovec, Sat Jun 6) and Gostilna Pri Planincu (Bled, Sun Jun 7) — call 1 day ahead.
  • Confirm rental car allows mountain roads (Vršič) and crossing into Italy.
  • Bring: warm layer for caves (12°C), sturdy walking shoes, ID/passport for Italy crossing, EasyPark app for parking, cash for some restaurants.
  • Book Škocjan Caves online for the 10:00 tour, Thu Jun 4 — postojnska-jama.eu. Predjama ticket can be bought on the spot at the castle.

Wednesday · 3 June
Land → Predjama → Piran walls at sunset
🌧️ 17–20° / 10–12° · Heavy rain

Plane lands 13:15, so this is an easy first day: one castle on the way, then settle into Piran and walk the walls in golden light.

  1. 13:15
    Ljubljana Airport (LJU)
    Land 13:15. Pick up rental car, aim to be on the road by ~14:00.
  2. 14:50
    Add-onPredjama Castle
    ~1h with audio tour. Castle built into a cliff cave, right on the airport→coast route. Open till ~19:00 in June.
  3. 17:00
    Piran (overnight)
    Park at Garage Fornače outside walls (~€20/day) — old town is car-free. Drop bags and settle in.
  4. 18:30
    Moved herePiran walls + old town
    €3 to climb the walls — best at golden hour. Tartini Square, St. George's bell tower. Dinner at Fritolin pri Cantini (open till 22:00, cash only).
2
Thursday · 4 June
Škocjan Caves → Italian loop
☀️ 22–23° / 11° · Clear

Caves first thing (booked tour), then west into Italy — Škocjan sits between Piran and Trieste, so no backtracking. Ends with sunset cliffs back in Slovenia.

  1. 08:30
    Leave Piran early
    Drive NE to the caves, ~40min. Aim to arrive by 09:30 to collect tickets.
  2. 10:00
    BookedŠkocjan Caves
    10:00 tour (book online — see checklist). ~2hrs, underground canyon. 12°C inside: bring a layer. No photos. Free parking. After: drive W to Italy (~40min, does NOT pass back through Piran).
  3. 13:00
    Muggia (lunch)
    Tiny Italian fishing village. Bring ID for border. Try Trattoria Risorta or any harbour-side spot. Wander the fortress ruins above.
  4. 14:30
    Trieste — Piazza Unità
    Park at Saba Trieste Centrale garage. Walk Unity Square, Canal Grande, coffee at Caffè degli Specchi.
  5. 16:30
    Miramare Castle
    Closes 18:30. Castle €12 / gardens free. Last entry ~17:30. Paid lot near entrance is easier than free parking chaos.
  6. 18:45
    Mesečev Zaliv (Moon Bay)
    Park at Strunjan church, 15min walk down. Dramatic flysch cliffs at golden hour. Sunset ~21:00 in June.
  7. 20:00
    Back to Piran for dinner
    EatTry Cantina (self-service, cheap wine €0.80) or Pirat Piran.
3
Friday · 5 June
Piran → Bovec via Tolmin & Slap Kozjak
☀️ up to 26° / 9° · Clear

The trip's longest drive (~3.5h with stops) but each stop earns it. Two of Slovenia's prettiest short walks.

  1. 09:00
    Leave Piran
    Drive northeast via highway, then off into the Soča Valley.
  2. 11:30
    Tolminska korita (Tolmin Gorges)
    €8 entry. Park at P2 (free, 15min walk in), not P1. Loop trail ~1hr. Buy ticket online to skip queue.
  3. 13:30
    LunchKobarid
    Hiša Polonka (casual local, ~€15 mains) or Picerija Fedrig (cheap pizza). Skip Hiša Franko unless you want a Michelin splurge.
  4. 15:00
    Hidden gemSlap Kozjak
    Park at Kobarid Fall lot. ~30min walk in past Napoleon Bridge. €5 final stretch. The most photogenic waterfall in Slovenia.
  5. 17:30
    Bovec (overnight)
    EatDinner at Gostilna pod Lipco — local trout, mushroom soup. Closed Tue (you're fine, it's Fri).
4
Saturday · 6 June
Bovec — Soča Valley
☀️ 21° / 10° · Clear

Pure "look at the river" day. Short walks, stunning water, mountain backdrop.

  1. 09:30
    Boka Waterfall
    Tallest in Slovenia (106m). Free pull-off near the bridge on road 203. 15min steep climb to viewpoint.
  2. 11:30
    Must-seeGreat Soča Gorge (Velika Korita)
    Free parking at trailhead. Walk upstream — turquoise water, suspension bridges. 2–3hrs out and back.
  3. 15:00
    Soča River viewpoints
    Several signed pullouts along road 203 between Bovec and Kobarid. Pick whichever looks good.
  4. 17:30
    Bovec evening
    Optional: Virje waterfall (5min walk) or Mlinarica Gorge. Otherwise rest up — tomorrow's drive is the highlight.
5
Sunday · 7 June
Bovec → Bled via Vršič Pass
⛰️ Bovec 24° → Vršič ~15°/5° → Bled 26° · big swing

The single most scenic driving day in Slovenia. 50 hairpins, 1,611m summit, alpine meadows, ending at the textbook lake.

  1. 09:00
    Leave Bovec, drive Vršič Pass
    Stop at numbered hairpin viewpoints. Lower gears on descent to save brakes. Cyclists everywhere.
  2. 10:00
    Russian Chapel + summit
    Built 1916 by Russian POWs. Free lot below chapel. Continue to summit (1,611m) for 360° views; coffee at Tičar or Erjavčeva koča.
  3. 12:00
    BonusLake Jasna (lunch)
    Cute alpine lake just below Vršič in Kranjska Gora. Free parking. Lunch at Milka or Restaurant Skipass.
  4. 14:30
    Must-seeSlap Peričnik
    Park at Mojstrana ski lot (€10/day, free shuttle) OR drive up gravel road (~€5). 10min walk. Walk behind it — best moment.
  5. 16:30
    Bled (overnight)
    EatPark at Hotel Park or Union garages. Dinner reserved at Gostilna Pri Planincu. Get Grandma Ivanka's goulash.
6
Monday · 8 June
Bled — gorge, lake, castle
☀️ 27° / 15° · Hot, peak day

Full Bled day, organized to chase good light: gorge in the morning, lake in the afternoon, castle for sunset.

  1. 09:00
    Pre-bookedVintgar Gorge
    Arrive on the dot of your timed ticket — they don't let you in early. Park free at Parking 2. ~2hrs total.
  2. 12:00
    Lake Bled walk + Pletna
    6km flat lakeside walk (~1.5hrs). Pletna boat to island ~€18pp, no booking, every 30min from Mlino. Try kremšnita.
  3. 17:00
    Bled Castle + Café Belvedere
    €19 castle entry — view alone is the win. Sunset hits the lake from up here. Café Belvedere at Vila Bled (short drive) for a different angle.
7
Tuesday · 9 June
Bohinj morning → Ljubljana
⛅ 28° / 16° · PM thunder risk

Bohinj is calmer and wilder than Bled. Two short walks, a hearty lunch, then drive to the capital.

  1. 08:30
    Slap Savica
    €3.50, drive to top parking. 25min steep walk up 558 steps — go early. Skip if knees are tired.
  2. 11:00
    Your pickMostnica Gorge
    From Stara Fužina. The prettiest part — heart-shaped section, "elephant" rock — is in the first 45min. 1.5–2hrs round trip is plenty.
  3. 13:30
    LunchGostilnica Štrudl
    Bohinjska Bistrica, 10min drive. Local žganci, bean stew, hearty Slovenian. ~€10–15 mains.
  4. 14:30
    Drive to Ljubljana
    ~1.5hrs via highway. Aim for old town arrival ~16:00.
  5. 16:00
    Ljubljana old town
    Park at Garaža Kongresni trg or NUK 2. Walk Triple Bridge → Ljubljanica river → Dragon Bridge. Castle funicular for sunset.
  6. 19:30
    Dinner
    CheapKlobasarna (sausages €5–7) · Olympia Burek (€3, late hours) · Šestica for sit-down · Špajza for nicer.

Wednesday · 10 June
Ljubljana → LJU airport
⛅ 18° → 26° · Light AM shower

Tight morning. Be at the airport ~08:45 to return car and clear security by 10:00.

  1. 07:30

Critical flags

  • Day 1 is wet. ~35 mm rain at the coast, ~19 mm inland. Pack a rain jacket and waterproof shoes in the carry-on, not the hold.
  • Day 5 has the biggest temperature swing of the trip. You'll leave Bovec at ~24°C, summit Vršič Pass at ~15°C max (possibly 5–8°C if you're up early), then arrive in Bled at 26°C. Fleece + windproof shell within reach in the car.
  • Day 6 = peak warmth (~27°C in Bled). Vintgar Gorge is shaded and feels noticeably cooler than ambient.
  • Day 7 afternoon has some thunderstorm risk in Ljubljana — keep an eye on ARSO.
